## Bugs and status
| # | Severity | Bug | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Pharmacy "Open at till" made an empty paper Rx even when the doctor's signed Rx was waiting | `pos.tag_visit` attaches the waiting signed Rx (and prints it per settings); paper only when none |
| 2 | High | Lab fees are paid at the lab by default, but the Lab technician role could not collect money | Default Lab technician gets `billing.payment.collect`; the lab row shows Collect or "Pay at cash" |
| 3 | Medium | "Still to pay" and the board ignored unpaid lab-order invoices | `flow.attach_lab_dues` adds them, with a pay link |
| 4 | Medium | Frequency box: typing TDS / BD / OD said "No match" | Dropdowns match option values too; an exact code match comes first |
| 5 | Low | Token lookup failed for M2, M002, "m 2" | `flow.normalize_token` |
| 6 | Low | Links that end in 403 (breadcrumbs, stock ledger sale links, lab dashboard, lab → consultation, visit papers → reception) | `can_open` / `{% openable %}`; lab dashboard flag; papers fall back to Close |
| 7 | Low | Pharmacy offered to tag visits the doctor had not seen | `visits_without_rx` lists only tokens in consultation or completed |
| 8 | **High** | Desk showed "Fee received now" from the hospital-wide switch while the fee invoice follows the **branch** rule: a branch billing at central cash took cash with **no invoice or payment recorded** | Desk follows `fee_counter(org, branch)`, shows "Paid at …, not here"; `register_visit` refuses to collect a fee billed elsewhere |
| 9 | Medium | Cancelling a token/appointment left its unpaid fee invoice outstanding forever; a paid fee got no refund prompt | `appointments.release_fee`: voids a fee with no money, warns to refund a paid one; cancelled visits drop their fee rows |
| 10 | Medium | A payment/deposit/staff-pay key already used on another record returned that record: the screen said "recorded" and nothing was | Keys are tied to their invoice, admission or person; a mismatch is refused |
| 11 | Low | Deactivating a role silently locked out everyone holding it | The message says how many active staff lose access |
| 12 | Low | Demo hospital had no staff pay profiles: payroll could not be demoed | `demo_data.SALARIES` → pay profiles in `seed_demo` |

WeasyPrint/fontTools INFO logging is set to WARNING (it filled the customer log). UX fixes in the same change: reception shows a Follow-up chip when the follow-up fee applies; the desk names the
counter that takes the fee when it is not reception.

## New: Payments by token
`/flow/token/?token=M-2` (and a box on the pharmacy Prescriptions page, lab worklist, doctor workspace and patient
flow board): today's bills for that token's patient in this branch (doctor's fee, lab, procedures, pharmacy), paid
and due, fees not billed yet, and the lab orders. Counters, cashiers and doctors; a doctor who is only a doctor sees
their own patients (others are a 404).

## Notes, not changed
- The toast can sit over the consultation's sticky sign bar for a few seconds.
- "Done here" at the pharmacy completes a visit without selling the Rx (a patient may decline medicine).
- A TDS order started in the afternoon offers only the 20:00 dose that day (by design: doses chart against their time).
