Reception

A patient joins the doctor's queue only once every fee paid at reception is paid. Emergencies go first and pay later.

{% include "components/field.html" with field=form.consultation_fee_billed_at %} {% include "components/field.html" with field=form.reception_prints_blank_rx %}

Other fees (injection, dressing...) are routed per service: Settings → Clinical lists → Services → "Fee is paid at".

Doctor, lab and pharmacy

After the doctor, a patient with tests to give goes to the lab first, then to the pharmacy if medicine or a pharmacy fee is waiting.
