# Mobile design — next phase

Design mobile workflows after desktop scope review. Reuse the same patient IDs, counter meanings, statuses, and design tokens.

## Staff app essentials
- Secure login and assigned role selection.
- Doctor: today's appointments, patient summary, history, results review, encounter drafts, follow-up tasks.
- Nurse: assigned beds, vitals capture, task checklist, shift handover, escalations.
- Admin: key daily metrics, actionable approvals, notifications.
- Pharmacy/lab: task status and authorized review notifications if operationally needed.

## Patient app / portal essentials
- Login and verified patient linkage.
- Book/reschedule appointments, upcoming visit, live token position.
- View released reports, signed prescriptions, discharge documents, receipts.
- Reminder preferences and account/privacy settings.

## Keep desktop-first initially
Cash closing, full stock receipt, batch reconciliation, role administration, advanced reports, and dense clinical editing.

## Mobile states to design
Offline/stale data, loading, failed submission, permission denied, session expiry, unread/acknowledged alert, and completed task. No confidential detail on lock-screen notifications by default. Patient and staff navigation remain separate.
